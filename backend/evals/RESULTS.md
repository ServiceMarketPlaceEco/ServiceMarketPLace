## 2. Fake-review detection

Test set: 54 reviews (22 fake, 32 genuine) across 12 providers over 60 days, scanned as one batch like the admin "Scan reviews" button.

| | Flagged | Not flagged |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Fake** | 15 (true positive) | 7 (missed) |
| **Genuine** | 4 (false alarm) | 28 (correctly left alone) |

**Precision 78.9% · Recall 68.2% · F1 0.73**

Missed fakes, by scenario:

- bought/dormant old account, unique text, no booking: f05, f06, f07
- friend books and completes a sham job, then reviews: f08, f09
- competitor smear from an old account: f12
- new account with a sham completed booking: f20

False alarms on genuine reviews, by scenario:

- brand-new customer, one-word review: g10
- busy day: several real jobs reviewed together: g16
- brand-new customer, provider no-show: g20
- genuine one-word review: g24
